Next: Automated Marketing

This is the point where I think we should move Adverio from a collection of useful features into a real marketing automation product.

The workflow should eventually look like:

1. Business profile
→ Adverio understands the business, services, location and target customers.

2. Create marketing campaign
→ AI generates the campaign strategy.

3. Generate content
→ Social posts, promotional emails, offers and other marketing content.

4. Schedule/publish
→ Content is scheduled rather than simply generated and copied manually.

5. Capture enquiries
→ Prospects submit an enquiry through the campaign.

6. AI lead response
→ Adverio responds to the lead.

7. Automated follow-up
→ If the lead doesn't convert, Adverio follows up at the appropriate time.

8. Conversion
→ Lead becomes a customer.

9. Analytics
→ Business owner can see which campaigns generated enquiries and customers.

So the real Adverio proposition becomes:

Adverio doesn't just create your marketing. It helps run it.



The next development stage

I would build the automation in this order:

Phase 1 — Lead automation

When a new lead arrives:

Save lead.
Identify campaign/business.
Generate an AI first-response message.
Create a follow-up record.
Show the lead as requiring attention.
Phase 2 — Automated follow-ups

Add a system that can determine:

follow-up due
follow-up sent
follow-up completed
follow-up cancelled
customer replied
Phase 3 — Marketing automation

Add automation around campaigns:

campaign activation
scheduled marketing content
campaign status
lead generation tracking
follow-up automation
campaign performance
Phase 4 — Automation controls

Inside Business Settings:

Marketing Automation

☑ Automatically generate lead responses
☑ Automatically create follow-ups
☑ Enable first follow-up
☑ Enable second follow-up
Follow-up timing
Default communication style

This starts turning Adverio from simply an AI marketing generator into an AI marketing automation platform.
